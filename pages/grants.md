**2026**: E-NICHE Short Term Scientific Mission 2026. Search for _Acyrthosiphon pisum_ populations adapted to wild pea relatives in Sicily. **2 k€**

**2025**: Scientific Installation Grant ("Aide à l'Installation Scientifique") from Rennes Métropole. Participation in the ILS5 international conference. **10 k€**

**2024**: GRATITUDE project, INRAE DIGIT-BIO meta-programme 2024. Development of digital phenotyping methods using computer vision. **20 k€**
